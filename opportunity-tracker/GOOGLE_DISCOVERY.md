# Indexed mentions and listing reviews

Implemented October 9, 2026. Requires the existing server-only ScrapeBadger key. Google is a discovery method: search snippets stay in a candidate registry; only retrieved original text enters conversation evidence. Sources retain their own publication date, author, URL and provider provenance. Unknown dates are never invented.

Reviewed mention keyword queries supply broad name/domain searches plus Reddit and Instagram site searches. Automatic discovery runs daily; the existing Growth keyword (15-minute), long-tail (6-hour) and AI (hourly) loops retain their own cadence. Historical searches extend the existing fixed 365-day job once, without resetting the job, grants, receipts, usage or review identities. Google searches use at most five pages per historical query and one page per recent query. Reaching a page/request/candidate cap reports partial coverage.

Discovered Reddit posts are hydrated and their comments read even when the post title lacks the business name. Direct Reddit comment-text search uses the existing Apify adapter, including both historical time boundaries. Original Instagram captions use ScrapeBadger media detail; account/hashtag pages and missing captions are not conversations. Other public pages use bounded original HTML retrieval. Unsupported sources remain explicit candidates, rather than fabricated evidence.

Google Maps collection verifies the listing name and exact official website domain through place detail, then pages newest written reviews to the historical boundary. Ratings without text and owner responses are excluded. App Store reviews bind to the exact official app ID and individual review ID. Listing-bound reviews can be verified mentions without repeating the business name. Namesake businesses and unverified name-only text remain unconfirmed. Maps exposes contributor identity rather than a native review permalink; stable identity includes listing and contributor, and the UI labels its source action **Open business listing**. App Store actions also open the listing.

All paid dispatches share the existing lease/CAS ledger and conservative uncertain-charge holds. Public provider pricing supplies reservations, including category defaults and near-term increases. ScrapeBadger's web endpoint also receives a hard six-credit request limit. Unknown pricing pauses affected tasks while other sources continue. Apify runs keep their existing actor charge and daily allowance limits; pilot Apify spend now consumes the same existing $2 total collection allowance, converted conservatively at 150 microUSD per credit. The $3 total AI and $2 daily AI limits are unchanged.

Coverage means the bounded queries and originals actually retrieved. Google indexing, provider availability, review date precision, page limits, source access and paid allowances prevent a claim of exhaustive coverage. Source failures, omitted records and unfinished review queues remain visible.

Historical Google queries are unrestricted by the indexed page's date. The fixed job window applies to each retrieved comment, caption, original article or review. This retains older threads with recent comments and undated business listings. Coverage version 3 reuses queued Google work and appends one corrective pass for already-searched dated queries, under the same job and allowances. New jobs record their coverage version immediately, preventing duplicate branch creation.
