You built the app. Find people it could help.

Tavern helps independent iOS developers find potential users, understand customer problems, and research the market around their apps. Review the evidence, decide where to join the conversation, and keep up with your sales along the way.

FIND RELEVANT CONVERSATIONS
Confirm what your app does and the problems it solves. Your lead board surfaces public Reddit discussions that may fit, explains the match, and links to the original post. Dismiss results that are not relevant.

TURN A MATCH INTO A THOUGHTFUL REPLY
Open a quest to review two example replies shaped to the request and Reddit community. Choose an approach, edit it in your own voice, and copy it before opening the discussion. You decide what to post.

RESEARCH YOUR MARKET
Research public discussions across Reddit, X, YouTube, forums, and reviews. Explore three views of your app's market:
• Problems: discover needs, frustrations, and workarounds, with sources to review.
• Landscape: research competing products, alternatives, and how people solve the problem today.
• People: review Reddit authors discussing the same or a similar problem, with context about whether the need was unresolved, later resolved, or unclear.

AI-assisted findings are starting points for your own research. Check the linked sources and dates before drawing conclusions or reaching out. Coverage and results vary; a match does not establish purchase intent.

FOLLOW SALES AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
Bring purchases, renewals, refunds, and other subscription updates together. Review sales by app and currency, and choose which events notify you. Hide amounts from notifications when you prefer.

CONNECT WHEN YOU ARE READY
Sign in with Apple, find your app, and confirm its profile. Sales tracking connects through App Store Server Notifications V2, directly or through RevenueCat's Apple-notification forwarding. No Tavern SDK is needed in your app.

FREE SALES TRACKING. OPTIONAL MARKETING.
Sales analytics and sales push notifications are free. Marketing subscriptions unlock ongoing lead discovery, suggested replies, and market insights for one app or up to three apps. Choose monthly or annual billing. There is no free trial. Prices and the total amount billed are shown in the app before purchase. Discovery and reply generation are subject to usage limits and source availability.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store subscription settings. Deleting your Tavern account does not cancel your subscription.

Explore demo without an account to browse clearly labeled sample leads, market insights, and sales.

Tavern is a companion for app developers. It does not process purchases, manage customer entitlements, or track paid app downloads. Sales amounts reflect available transaction information, not App Store payouts or net proceeds. Notifications depend on received events, network availability, and device settings.
