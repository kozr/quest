Questline is a free companion for iOS app developers who want to view and receive alerts for their apps' App Store Server Notifications. It does not sell digital content or subscriptions in this version.

REVIEW WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
On the welcome screen, tap Explore demo. The welcome screen opens immediately without a service connection or server configuration. This is a public, offline feature available to every user. It contains clearly labeled sample apps and events, including purchases, renewal, refund, free-trial, and auto-renew changes. Open event details, inspect sample apps in the Apps tab, and try Settings alert preferences. Settings includes an on-screen sample notification; toggling Hide amounts in notifications updates that preview. Sample changes are temporary and never send pushes or modify a real account. Tap Settings > Exit demo to return to sign-in. Demo mode stays separate from live accounts, QR pairing, and remote notifications.

LIVE ACCOUNT FLOW
Sign in with Apple to create an account. The service address is configured by the app; reviewers never need to enter a server URL. No separate username or password exists. Allow the notification prompt after sign-in; the phone registers automatically. In Settings, use Send test push to test your device. If notifications were previously denied, use Open notification settings to enable them, then return to Questline. Apps > Add app offers On a computer (Recommended) or On this iPhone. On a computer provides two steps to share/open the dashboard and scan its sign-in QR after closing the chooser. On this iPhone opens the setup page inside the same sheet, already signed in. The web flow is Find, Confirm, Connect: find a published app by name, App Store URL, or Apple ID; confirm its identity; then choose Apple or RevenueCat and follow the connection steps. Optional shortcuts, paste, and manual entry for unpublished apps are under Can’t find your app?. Select an app > Manage connection to open its notification URLs, forwarding settings, and optional Apple connection tests. Close the sheet to refresh the native app. Provider shortcuts open App Store Connect or RevenueCat; no computer or QR scan is needed. Optional desktop access at https://quest-liart-iota.vercel.app uses Settings > Sign in on computer to scan and approve the browser QR. Connecting a real developer app requires its own App Store Server Notifications V2 configuration. Real Apple notifications are signature-verified before being stored or forwarded.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Delete account explains the data removed and asks the user to confirm with the same Apple Account. Apple authorization is revoked before durable cleanup is accepted. All service sessions and notification endpoints stop working immediately after acceptance, and cleanup continues if the app closes. Reopen the app to check completion. Users with existing forwarded endpoints are told to restore their App Store Server Notification URLs before deleting.

Camera access is optional and used only for local QR scanning; manual paste is available. Push permission is requested automatically after sign-in if the user has not already made a choice. Signed-out users and demo mode are not prompted. Notifications remain optional. The privacy policy and support links are available both before sign-in and in Settings.
