Tavern is a marketing and sales companion for independent iOS developers. It adds Leads, suggested replies, and Market research to sales analytics and alerts.

PUBLIC OFFLINE DEMO
Tap Explore demo on the welcome screen. No account or network connection is required. Fictional information is labeled Demo or Sample. Browse Activity, Leads, Market, Apps, and Settings. Leads > Pick Up Quest shows example replies to expand, edit, and copy; opening a sample discussion shows a sample notice. Market includes Problems, Landscape, and People. Activity offers sample sales and the ledger. Demo does not send pushes, make purchases, or post online. Exit demo in Settings to test a live account.

LIVE ACCOUNT AND MARKETING SUBSCRIPTIONS
Sign in with Apple; there is no separate reviewer password. Find a published app and confirm its problems and capabilities. Profiles support public-conversation discovery; scans may take time or return no matches.

Sales analytics and sales push notifications are free. Marketing uses Apple auto-renewable subscriptions for one connected app or up to three, with monthly or annual billing and no free trial. The paywall displays localized StoreKit prices, the total billed amount, renewal information, Restore purchases, Terms, and Privacy. Settings > Explore Marketing / Manage Marketing opens that screen. Exercise purchases and restoration using Apple's review purchase environment; the offline demo does not test billing.

AI AND PUBLIC SOURCES
OpenAI processes public App Store metadata, the confirmed app profile, and public source evidence to suggest profiles, find leads, research the market, and draft replies. Profile confirmation explains sharing with OpenAI. Public source evidence can include author handles, names, links, and post images; it is not anonymous. Account credentials, device tokens, sales transactions, and Marketing purchase records are not intentionally included in AI requests. Requests disable response storage; provider retention is described in the privacy policy.

Findings are AI-assisted suggestions, not verified purchase intent. Review source links and dates. The user edits and copies replies; Tavern does not post or contact authors. External sources open on their own websites. Demo research is illustrative.

SALES SETUP AND PUSH
Connect App Store Server Notifications V2 directly or through RevenueCat's forwarding of Apple's notifications (not RevenueCat webhooks). No Tavern SDK is required. A live sales connection requires an app the reviewer controls; a saved URL or sandbox event does not prove a production connection. Notification permission is optional. Settings > Send test push queues an alert; acceptance is not proof of delivery. Optional computer sign-in uses a QR approval with on-device camera scanning or manual paste. Camera images are not uploaded.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Settings > Delete account requires fresh confirmation with the same Apple Account. Apple authorization is revoked before cleanup is accepted. Reopen the app to check completion. Restore any forwarding endpoints before deletion. Deleting a Tavern account does not cancel Apple subscription billing; cancellation is managed in App Store subscription settings.
